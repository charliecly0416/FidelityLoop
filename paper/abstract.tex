Replay models guide elastic LLM-serving policies for online requests and deadline-constrained offline jobs sharing GPUs, but accurate timing and aggregate resource estimates need not preserve deployment decisions. We present FidelityLoop, a closed-loop framework aligning replay and physical execution through shared policy interfaces, request identities, and lifecycle accounting. It supports rule-based policies and PPO training in replay, followed by frozen-policy validation on vLLM/SGLang; Vidur supplies an alternative replay service model. Measurements on two A100 GPUs and controlled replay contrasts reveal two failure
mechanisms: startup timing can cross a controller’s cooldown boundary and alter subsequent actions, while
resource errors can cancel in aggregate despite temporal misalignment. A historical startup proxy falsely rejects eight of 22 physically feasible SGLang runs. Retrospective analysis shows replay-based selection choosing a policy that fails all six
physical executions across two windows; in another tested pool, it chooses a feasible policy costing 58.95\% more than the
lowest-cost feasible alternative under the scenario ledger. Validation also covers AWQ-quantized execution and active four-replica control. On full-day variable-length inputs, host-calibrated replay falsely accepts a guarded policy that misses physical offline deadlines. Request-aligned traces connect timing discrepancies to control actions and missed deadlines. We release paired traces from 197 physical runs, including 36 preregistered runs, companion records for 44 additional executions, CPU
analysis tools, and fixed-input Vidur replay under an explicit resource and synthetic-API cost contract.
