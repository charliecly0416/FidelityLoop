Replay models guide elastic LLM-serving policies for online requests and deadline-constrained offline jobs sharing GPUs, but accurate timing and aggregate resource estimates need not preserve deployment decisions. We present FidelityLoop, a closed-loop framework aligning replay and physical execution through shared policy interfaces, request identities, and lifecycle accounting. It supports rule-based policies and PPO training in replay, followed by frozen-policy validation on vLLM/SGLang; Vidur supplies an alternative replay service model. Measurements on two A100 GPUs and controlled replay contrasts reveal two mechanisms: startup timing can cross a controller's cooldown boundary and alter subsequent actions, while resource errors can cancel in aggregate despite temporal misalignment. Retrospective selection chooses a policy failing all six physical executions in one pool and a feasible policy costing 58.95\% more than the cheapest feasible alternative in another. We then freeze predictions and choices before 36 executions with actual HTTP inference. Host-calibrated replay, a fixed 30\,s proxy, and a timer diagnostic select the cheapest consistently feasible candidate in the eight-policy pool; reusing the archived startup estimate selects one costing 19.87\% more, including provider GPU capacity. On full-day arrivals, locally calibrated replay still accepts a guarded policy that misses offline deadlines; model and four-replica studies test transfer. Request-aligned traces connect timing discrepancies to actions and deadlines. We release paired traces from 197 historical physical runs and companion records for the deployment extensions, and CPU analysis tools under explicit study-specific cost contracts.
