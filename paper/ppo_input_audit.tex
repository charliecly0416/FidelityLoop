\subsection{Online source-row isolation audit}
\label{app:input-row-audit}
We compare online source-row hashes across three training, three validation,
and two deployment inputs. All 28 pairwise intersections are empty; the three
validation-file hashes match the frozen training contract. Table~\ref{tab:input-row-audit}
gives the included row counts and raw timestamp ranges in seconds from the
source's time origin. Deployment hashes cover the retained online entries in the source-row maps,
matching the generated online arrivals. Repeated hashes within
a file explain the difference between row and unique-hash counts.
